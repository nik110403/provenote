# Labour flexibility and financial crises (demo draft)

Across 130 countries, flexible labour markets soften output losses after a financial crisis [@lei2023financial].

The authors stress that "flexibility cushions every economy, including the eurozone, by roughly 4 percent of GDP" [@lei2023financial].

In developed countries the buffer disappears inside the eurozone, and works only outside it [@lei2023financial].

As one commentator put it, "labour reform is the only free lunch left in macroeconomics".

# lei2023financial

Lei, Li and Mei (2023, PLOS ONE) estimate local projections of real GDP on a financial-crisis dummy, a labor-market-flexibility index (Fraser Institute hiring and firing regulation) and their interaction, on a panel of 130 countries from 2000 to 2019. The flexibility buffer is found in developing countries and in non-eurozone developed countries, not in the eurozone. A mechanism analysis places the effect in the non-tradable sector, in particular transport, storage and communication and the other-industries group. Robustness checks add controls, drop 2008 and 2009, and swap in a World Economic Forum flexibility proxy. Crises are treated as exogenous shocks, which the authors use to sidestep reverse causality.
